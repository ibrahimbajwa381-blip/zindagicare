================================================================================
WHAT IS ZINDAGICARE?
================================================================================

ZindagiCare is a healthcare platform for Pakistan that connects patients with
PMDC-verified doctors. It offers three main services:

  1. Paid online video consultations (patients book a time slot and pay online)
  2. Free Sunday video consultations (10 minutes, Rs. 0 — one slot per patient per week)
  3. In-home medical care in Lahore (doctor or nurse visits the patient's home)

The platform also includes doctor verification, patient messaging, pre-consult
medical history forms, and an admin team console to manage the whole operation.

Current launch focus: Lahore only. Prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).
The website works in both English and Urdu.

SECTION 1 — REQUIREMENTS THAT ARE DONE
================================================================================

These features are built and can be used today (some may need live accounts
for payments, video, or SMS before going public — see Section 2).


--- A. GETTING DOCTORS ON THE PLATFORM ---

[✓] Doctor registration and login (email, Google sign-in, phone OTP in test mode)
[✓] Doctor onboarding — city, language, date of birth, gender
[✓] PMDC verification workflow:
      - Doctor submits PMDC registration number, CNIC, degree, PMDC certificate
      - Admin team reviews documents and approves or rejects
      - Approved doctors get a "PMDC verified" badge on their profile
[✓] Doctor profile setup:
      - Specialty, consultation fee, bio, photo
      - Hospital/clinic locations
      - Weekly availability (working hours)
      - Optional Sunday free consult hours
      - Languages spoken
[✓] Admin can create doctor accounts directly and manage listings


--- B. GETTING PATIENTS ON THE PLATFORM ---

[✓] Patient registration and login
[✓] Patient onboarding (city, language, basic details)
[✓] Patient dashboard — upcoming appointments, history, home visits, messages
[✓] Public doctor directory — browse doctors by specialty, see fees and ratings
[✓] Individual doctor profile pages with booking option


--- C. PAID ONLINE VIDEO CONSULTATION (Main Revenue Service) ---

[✓] Patient selects doctor and available time slot
[✓] Checkout and payment step (test/demo payments work now; JazzCash ready to turn on)
[✓] Private video consultation room (via Zoom — needs Zoom account to go live)
[✓] Doctor sees incoming consult requests and can join the video call
[✓] Doctor can see list of past patients from paid consults
[✓] Appointment confirmation and status tracking


--- D. SUNDAY FREE CONSULT (Key Differentiator) ---

[✓] Rs. 0, ~10-minute video consults every Sunday
[✓] One free slot per patient per week (first come, first served)
[✓] Doctors opt in and set their Sunday hours
[✓] Live queue for doctors — mark patient as completed or no-show
[✓] Real-time slot updates — patients see availability as slots fill up
[✓] Rules to prevent abuse:
      - Short hold timer when reserving a slot
      - No-show tracking
      - Temporary ban for patients who repeatedly miss appointments
[✓] Same video room experience as paid consults


--- E. PRE-CONSULT MEDICAL HISTORY (Intake) ---

[✓] Standard health questions in English AND Urdu:
      - Chief complaint, current medications, allergies, chronic conditions, etc.
[✓] Doctors can customize their own question list in profile settings
[✓] Patients can fill intake via a dedicated form page
[✓] For paid online consults: intake questions can appear as a chat-based Q&A
[✓] Doctors see a structured summary before/during the consultation


--- F. PATIENT–DOCTOR MESSAGING ---

[✓] Secure chat between patient and doctor after a paid consultation
[✓] Real-time message delivery
[✓] Unread message counts
[✓] Doctors can start conversations with patients from past paid consults
[✓] Search within conversations

Note: Messaging opens after paid consults. Sunday free consult patients do not
automatically get ongoing chat access.


--- G. IN-HOME MEDICAL SERVICES (Lahore) ---

[✓] Patients can request home visits for:
      - Doctor home visit
      - General nurse visit
      - IV drip
      - Injection
      - Wound care
[✓] Fixed PKR prices shown upfront before booking
      (e.g. doctor visit ~Rs. 3,500 | nurse visit ~Rs. 1,800)
[✓] System auto-assigns nearest available nurse or doctor (within ~15 km of Lahore)
[✓] Visit status tracking: requested → assigned → on the way → arrived → completed
[✓] Nurse/doctor can upload a visit report after completion
[✓] Admin can view all home visits and manually assign if needed


--- H. NURSE WORKSPACE ---

[✓] Nurse registration and verification (nursing license, CNIC documents)
[✓] Availability on/off toggle
[✓] Visit dashboard with status updates
[✓] Visit report upload
[✓] Profile and service area settings


--- I. DOCTOR WORKSPACE ---

[✓] Dashboard with overview stats (patients, requests, Sunday queue, home visits)
[✓] Profile editing (fee, hours, hospitals, photo, intake questions)
[✓] Sections for: messages, my patients, consult requests, Sunday consult, home visits


--- J. ADMIN / OPERATIONS CONSOLE ---

[✓] Dashboard overview
[✓] Doctor verification queue and doctor management
[✓] Nurse verification queue
[✓] Patient list
[✓] All appointments — view, cancel, reassign
[✓] Sunday consult admin tools
[✓] Home care dispatch management
[✓] Broadcast in-app notifications to users


--- K. PAKISTAN-SPECIFIC ELEMENTS (Already Built) ---

[✓] Full Urdu language across public website and patient flows
[✓] Language switcher (English / Urdu)
[✓] Lahore-first positioning in marketing and service area
[✓] All scheduling in Pakistan Standard Time (Karachi timezone)
[✓] All prices in PKR (Pakistani Rupees)
[✓] Pakistani mobile number format (03xx) supported
[✓] CNIC fields for doctor and nurse verification
[✓] PMDC verification as part of doctor onboarding (manual admin review)

SECTION 2 — REQUIREMENTS THAT ARE PARTIALLY DONE
================================================================================

These exist in the system but are not fully ready for a smooth public launch.
They need configuration, finishing work, or a business decision before patients
and doctors can rely on them.


--- PAYMENTS ---

[~] JazzCash (Pakistan's popular mobile wallet):
      Built end-to-end, but turned OFF by default.
      Platform currently uses test/demo checkout for demonstrations.
      Needs: JazzCash merchant account + credentials to accept real money.

[~] EasyPaisa and card payments (Stripe):
      Mentioned in system design only. No working checkout yet.

[~] Home care payments:
      Price is shown to patient at booking, but there is no online payment step.
      Likely cash on delivery or manual settlement for now.


--- VIDEO CONSULTATIONS ---

[~] Zoom video rooms:
      Fully built in the app, but requires a Zoom business account and API
      credentials. Without setup, the "Join Video" button will not work in
      production.


--- SMS, WHATSAPP, AND EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS ---

[~] SMS for OTP login, booking confirmations, and reminders:
      Code is ready, but no live SMS provider is connected yet.
      Messages currently only appear in server logs during testing.

[~] WhatsApp reminders:
      Referenced in the system but not connected to any WhatsApp service.
      (WhatsApp is very important for patient communication in Pakistan.)

[~] Email notifications:
      Basic support exists; reliability for production not fully tested.


--- DIGITAL PRESCRIPTIONS (E-Rx) ---

[~] The website promises digital prescriptions to patients.
[~] The database can store prescription files.
[~] BUT: There is no screen for doctors to write or upload a prescription.
[~] AND: There is no screen for patients to view or download their prescription.
      This is a major gap for a complete consult experience.


--- PATIENT REVIEWS AND RATINGS ---

[~] Doctor profiles can display star ratings and reviews IF data exists.
[~] Patients cannot yet submit a review after their consultation.
      Only the "display" side is built, not the "submit" side.


--- CLINIC / IN-PERSON APPOINTMENTS ---

[~] The system understands "clinic visit" as an appointment type.
[~] Doctors can see clinic-type requests in their dashboard.
[~] Patients cannot book an in-clinic appointment through the app today.
      Only online paid consult and Sunday free consult booking exist.


--- MEDICAL INTAKE ON SUNDAY CONSULTS ---

[~] Intake works well for paid online consults (form + chat Q&A).
[~] Sunday booking reminds patients about medical history, but the full
      chat-based intake flow is mainly tied to paid appointments.
      Sunday patients may not get the same guided intake experience.


--- NURSE DASHBOARD LANGUAGE ---

[~] Patient-facing website and marketing: fully bilingual (English + Urdu).
[~] Nurse dashboard: mostly English-only labels. Needs Urdu translation.


--- HOME CARE LOCATION ACCURACY ---

[~] Patient enters address as text.
[~] If patient does not share GPS location, system uses a default central
      Lahore point — which may affect how accurately the nearest nurse is matched.
[~] Google Maps integration is configured but not deeply built into the booking form.


--- RIDER / MEDICINE DELIVERY ---

[~] The system has a "rider" role in its design for future medicine delivery.
[~] No rider registration, app, or delivery workflow exists yet.


--- FAMILY MEMBER BOOKING ---

[~] Database supports booking a consult for a family member (child, elderly parent).
[~] No patient-facing screen to add family members or book on their behalf.


--- PRODUCTION READINESS (Behind the Scenes) ---

[~] Phone OTP and slot reservations use temporary in-memory storage in development.
      For high traffic at launch, a more robust setup (Redis) is recommended.
[~] Real-time updates use a lightweight method suitable for MVP, but may need
      upgrading if thousands of users book Sunday slots at the same time.

SECTION 3 — REQUIREMENTS STILL PENDING (NOT BUILT YET)
================================================================================

These are needed for a "complete" MVP that matches what patients in Pakistan
expect from a modern healthcare platform — but they have not been built yet.


--- CORE CLINICAL & PATIENT EXPERIENCE ---

[ ] E-prescriptions:
      Doctor writes or uploads prescription after consult;
      patient receives and can download/share it (PDF or digital format).

[ ] Patient review submission:
      After a consult, patient rates the doctor and leaves feedback.

[ ] Clinic booking for patients:
      Book a physical in-clinic visit at a doctor's hospital through the app.

[ ] Family / dependent profiles:
      One account manages appointments for children, spouse, or elderly parents.

[ ] Medical intake fully integrated with Sunday free consults:
      Same guided health history experience as paid consults.


--- PAYMENTS (PAKISTAN) ---

[ ] EasyPaisa checkout (very widely used in Pakistan alongside JazzCash)
[ ] Card payments for patients who prefer debit/credit card
[ ] Online payment for home care visits (not just cash on delivery)


--- COMMUNICATION (CRITICAL FOR PAKISTAN MARKET) ---

[ ] Live SMS OTP:
      Reliable phone-number login without needing email.
      (Most Pakistani patients prefer phone over email.)

[ ] WhatsApp appointment reminders and confirmations:
      Booking confirmed, 24-hour reminder, 1-hour reminder, Sunday morning nudge.
      WhatsApp is the primary communication channel for most patients in Pakistan.

[ ] Mobile push notifications:
      Alerts on phone home screen (requires a mobile app or PWA setup).


--- PHARMACY & MEDICINE DELIVERY (Phase 2 — Promised in Marketing) ---

[ ] Online pharmacy / medicine catalog
[ ] Order medicines based on doctor's prescription
[ ] Rider/delivery staff app and dispatch system
[ ] Order tracking for patients


--- HOME CARE EXPANSION ---

[ ] Sample collection at home (blood tests, etc.)
[ ] Emergency/urgent home visits (listed in marketing as "coming soon")
[ ] Live GPS tracking of nurse/doctor on the way (like Careem/InDrive model)
[ ] Integrated map picker for patient address (not just text address)


--- TRUST & COMPLIANCE ---

[ ] Automated PMDC registry verification:
      Today an admin manually checks uploaded certificates.
      Ideal: system checks PMDC number against official registry automatically.

[ ] Patient consent and medical data privacy documentation (Urdu + English)
[ ] Terms of service and refund policy pages finalized for public launch
[ ] Data protection compliance aligned with Pakistani healthcare norms


--- GEOGRAPHIC EXPANSION ---

[ ] Multi-city support beyond Lahore (Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, etc.)
      Currently locked to Lahore only.


--- DOCTOR EXPERIENCE GAPS ---

[ ] Doctor earnings dashboard (how much earned, pending payouts, history)
[ ] Automated payout to doctor's bank account or JazzCash
[ ] Doctor can issue follow-up appointment or referral from within the app
[ ] Voice-only consult option (for patients with poor internet)


--- OPERATIONS & GROWTH ---

[ ] Marketing landing pages finalized for paid ad campaigns (Facebook, Google)
[ ] Referral program (patient refers patient, doctor refers doctor)
[ ] Customer support chat or helpline integration
[ ] Analytics dashboard for admin (bookings, revenue, no-show rate, etc.)

SECTION 4 — WHAT THIS MEANS FOR LAUNCH IN PAKISTAN
================================================================================

WHAT YOU CAN USE TODAY (Soft Launch / Doctor Testing):
  - Onboard as a doctor with PMDC documents and get listed after admin approval
  - Set your consultation fee, working hours, hospital locations
  - Offer optional free Sunday consult slots
  - Receive paid online consult bookings (with test payments; JazzCash when live)
  - Run Sunday free consults with a proper live queue
  - See patient medical history (English/Urdu) before the video call
  - Chat with patients after paid consults
  - Accept home visit assignments if enabled
  - Customize your own intake questions

WHAT PATIENTS CAN DO TODAY:
  - Register, browse doctors, book paid online consult or Sunday free slot
  - Fill medical history form before consult
  - Join video call (once Zoom is configured)
  - Request in-home nurse or doctor visit in Lahore
  - Message their doctor after a paid consult

WHAT IS NOT READY FOR PUBLIC LAUNCH YET:
  - Issuing digital prescriptions through the app
  - Patients leaving reviews after consults
  - Booking in-clinic appointments at your hospital
  - Reliable SMS/WhatsApp reminders (patients may miss appointments)
  - EasyPaisa or card payments
  - Medicine delivery after prescription
  - Cities other than Lahore

NOTES FOR DOCTOR PARTNER
================================================================================

1. The platform is substantially built. The core doctor–patient journey for
   online consults and Sunday free consults works. Home nursing in Lahore works.

2. The biggest gaps before calling this a "perfect MVP" are:
   - Real payments (JazzCash live)
   - Live video (Zoom configured)
   - Patient communication (SMS + WhatsApp)
   - Digital prescriptions after every consult
   - Patient reviews to build trust

3. PMDC verification is currently manual — your admin team reviews uploaded
   documents. This is acceptable for early launch with a small doctor panel,
   but does not scale without automation.

4. The Sunday free consult is a strong differentiator for Pakistan. Few
   platforms offer structured, free weekly consults with queue management and
   no-show rules. Protect and promote this feature.

5. Lahore-only launch is intentional and correct. Expand only after home care
   logistics and doctor supply are proven in one city.

6. This document should be reviewed together after each development sprint and
   updated as items move from Pending → Partial → Done.

1. THE PROBLEM WE STARTED WITH
------------------------------
A patient booked a doctor, opened the chat, and the chat disappeared after
2-3 seconds ("No conversations yet").

Root cause:
- Opening a chat (POST /api/v1/chat/threads) checks both sides of an account
  ("you as doctor" and "you as patient"), so the chat opens.
- The chat list (GET /api/v1/chat/threads) checks only ONE side: if the
  account has the "doctor" role it only looks for patients who booked that
  doctor. The thread is filtered out.
- The inbox refreshes every 8 seconds, replaces the list, and the open chat
  vanishes.
- This only happens to accounts holding BOTH "patient" and "doctor" roles.
  The account used for testing (Ibrahim Aslam Bajwa) had
  patient + doctor + physiotherapist, which confirms the cause.

Why two-role accounts still existed: one-role-per-account was only enforced
for NEW sign-ups. Older accounts were never fixed, and the database did not
reject two roles.

8. NEW FEATURE: RING TO JOIN A VIDEO CONSULT (NOT COMMITTED YET)
----------------------------------------------------------------
If the other person has not joined the video call, the doctor can "ring"
the patient (and the patient can ring the doctor), like a WhatsApp call.

How it works:
- In the video call header there is a new "Ring patient" / "Ring doctor"
  button (bell icon only on phones). You must be in the call to ring.
- The ring repeats every ~7 seconds for about 45 seconds. A banner shows
  "Ringing <name>...", then "They're joining now", "Call declined" or
  "No answer". Tapping the button again, or leaving the call, stops it.
- The other person:
  - App open on any page: full-screen incoming call screen with photo,
    ringtone, vibration, and Join / Decline buttons.
  - PWA closed or in the background: a call notification that stays on
    screen, vibrates, and has Join / Decline buttons. Repeated pushes keep
    the phone buzzing for the 45 seconds. Decline works from the
    notification.
  - Native mobile app: gets a push; tapping it opens the web call page.
- Joining the call marks the ring as answered and stops it on both sides.

Limits:
- No custom ringtone when the PWA is closed (browsers do not allow it);
  the phone's normal notification sound and vibration are used instead.
- iPhone: push only works if the PWA is installed to the home screen; no
  vibration pattern or Join/Decline buttons, but tapping opens the call.
- The other person must have notifications turned on, or have the app
  open, to see the ring.

9. SHARED CHAT AND CONSULTATION SYSTEM - BUILT (NOT COMMITTED YET)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 7's plan is now built: phases 1, 2 and 3. Herbalist and nutritionist
online booking is still for later.

How it works now:
- Chat never looks at account roles. Who can talk to whom comes only from
  real consultations and home visits (who is the patient, who is the
  provider). The "chat disappears" bug cannot come back, even if two-role
  accounts reappear.
- Chat has no stored open/closed flag. It is worked out each time:
    Visible:  the pair has had an eligible consult or visit (history stays
              readable).
    Writable: a consult is booked or in progress, or a home visit is
              assigned / en route / arrived, and it is not more than 72
              hours past its scheduled time.
    Completed, cancelled or no-show -> read-only (or hidden if never valid).
- Paid and Sunday free consults follow exactly the same rules.
- One chat thread per patient-provider pair. A new booking reopens it.
- Works for doctors, herbalists, nutritionists, nurses and physiotherapists.
